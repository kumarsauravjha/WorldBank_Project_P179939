Key Insights:

  • Egypt emerges as Africa's most efficient export gateway to five of six global regions, demonstrating that regional hub development can significantly reduce transport costs.
  • Egypt's extraordinary cross-regional advantage suggests transferable best practices that could be implemented in other African regions.
  • Cape Verde shows Atlantic advantage for North American trade that remains underutilized, representing a significant opportunity for West Africa.
  • Strategic port development in other regions could create similar efficiencies, reducing logistics costs across the continent.
