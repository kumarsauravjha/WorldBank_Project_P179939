The Volume Challenge

Relationship between Shipment Volume and Cost per Ton

Central Message: "Africa's smaller trade volumes compound its geographic disadvantage."

Key Insights:

  • Small shipments (0-10 tons) from Africa face extremely high costs ($6,642/ton) - This is significantly higher than the already high global average for small shipments.
  • Low and lower-middle income countries (most African nations) pay 49% more in logistics costs than high-income countries.
  • Economies of scale are not being achieved due to fragmented supply chains - costs drop dramatically as volume increases.
  • Volume consolidation represents a significant opportunity for immediate cost reduction - combining small shipments could reduce costs by up to 80%.

Data source: World Bank Logistics Analysis 2023. Visualization shows regression curve based on global average costs by shipment volume bracket.
